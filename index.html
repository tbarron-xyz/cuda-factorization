Bound-Splitting and Next-Candidate Demo

This page demonstrates the bound-splitting and next-candidate algorithms from the cuda-factorization GitHub repository, which implements GPU kernels for parallel factorization enumeration in numerical semigroups.

How to Use

Enter the element to factorize and the 5 generators (g1 to g5). Click "Start Lexicographic Enumeration" to begin with one worker that searches from the initial candidate down to the lower bound [0,0,0,0,0].

Each worker displays its lower bound, current candidate, and finished status. Click "Split" on an active worker to create a new worker with a tighter lower bound, allowing parallel enumeration.

Click "Next Candidate" to advance all active workers to their next lexicographic candidate.
